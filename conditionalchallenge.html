HOW TO PLAY

Hacko moves continuously through the maze.

Press a direction before reaching an intersection. The queued direction will activate when the path becomes available.

Use the arrow keys, W, A, S, and D, or tap the on-screen controller.

Collect the glowing clue chips required for the mission.

Large power orbs temporarily make the security birds vulnerable.

Reach the bomb chamber and press E or tap the yellow E button.

Select the correct wire by evaluating the conditional statement.

You have two minutes to reach the bomb. Opening it starts a 30-second defuse countdown - cut the right wire before it reaches zero.
